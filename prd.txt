# 📄 Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Name (Working Title): CycleSync AI

---

## 1. Product Overview

### 🎯 Vision

Build a web-based AI assistant that tracks, predicts, and personalizes menstrual cycle insights — including cycle phases, diet, exercise, and supplement recommendations — tailored to age, health status, menstrual history, and conditions like PCOS/PCOD.

### 🚩 Problem Statement

Most period tracking apps:

* Focus only on logging and basic prediction.
* Provide generic advice.
* Do not personalize recommendations for women with PCOS/PCOD.
* Do not integrate holistic lifestyle guidance (diet + training + supplementation + hormonal phases).

Users need:

* Accurate cycle predictions.
* Phase-based lifestyle recommendations.
* Customization for irregular cycles and PCOS.
* A simple web-based assistant without heavy app installs.

---

## 2. Goals & Non-Goals

### ✅ Goals (MVP)

1. Track menstrual history.
2. Predict next cycle and phases.
3. Identify irregular cycles and possible PCOS patterns.
4. Generate personalized recommendations:

   * Diet
   * Exercise
   * Supplements
5. Deliver recommendations through an AI assistant interface.
6. Work entirely via Firebase (Firestore + Auth) with no custom backend.

### ❌ Non-Goals (MVP)

* No medical diagnosis.
* No hormone lab integration.
* No wearable integrations.
* No teleconsultation.
* No native mobile app.
* No advanced ML training pipeline (rule-based + lightweight AI only).

---

## 5.4 Cycle Prediction Logic

### For Regular Cycles

* Average last 3–6 cycles.
* Predict next period.
* Divide into 4 phases:

  * Menstrual
  * Follicular
  * Ovulatory
  * Luteal

### For Irregular / PCOS

* Use weighted average + standard deviation.
* Flag highly irregular cycles (>35 days or variable >7 days).
* Ovulation window estimated probabilistically.
* Confidence score shown to user.

Prediction logic runs client-side (JavaScript).

## 10. Metrics for Success

### MVP KPIs

* D7 retention > 35%

* 60% users engaging with recommendations
* 40% repeat usage per week

## 11. Risks

1. Irregular cycle prediction in PCOS may be inaccurate.
2. Over-promising medical accuracy.
3. Supplement recommendations risk.
4. Data privacy concerns.

Mitigation:

* Confidence score.
* Soft language.
* Clear disclaimers.
* Secure Firebase rules.
